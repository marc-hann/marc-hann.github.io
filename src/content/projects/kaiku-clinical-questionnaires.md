Kaiku's questionnaires weren't standardised. Each clinic and hospital had its own requirements for radiotherapy, chemotherapy and immunotherapy follow-up, and someone had to turn them into questionnaires patients could use.

I did this throughout my time at Kaiku, and until Elekta acquired the company in 2020 I was practically the only one doing it. I worked directly with clinics to define their questionnaires. Some were based on the PRO-CTCAE standard, others written for a single clinic. When a clinic needed something the [questionnaire engine](#kaiku-questionnaire-engine) didn't support and the change was feasible, I extended it.

The result was questionnaires clinics put into use with their patients in the Kaiku app.
