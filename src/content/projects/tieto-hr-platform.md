An internal system that handles everything related to HR for a large company, built during my time as a consultant at TietoEvry.

The company works in a regulated industry, so the system had to meet compliance requirements on top of everything a typical HR tool does.

It was a large project for a team of two engineers. I designed the product and built the frontend and part of the backend.

The system is in use today. The client is confidential.
