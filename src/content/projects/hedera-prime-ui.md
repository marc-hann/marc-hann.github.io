Once the [design system](#hedera-design-system) was in place, I rebuilt the entire user interface of [Hedera Prime](#hedera-prime-mvp) on top of it and shaped much of the product's UX.

The new interface covers the whole sample-to-report flow labs work through, from checking that a sequencing run performed as expected to assessing variants and building the clinical report.

The challenge was the transition. The old and new interfaces had to run side by side throughout development, and keeping every feature working in both took extra care.
