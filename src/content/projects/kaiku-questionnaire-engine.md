Kaiku Health helps cancer patients report symptoms to their care team between hospital visits, through questionnaires in the Kaiku app. Many questionnaires were shared between clinics, but each clinic needed its own content, in its own language.

In my last year at Kaiku I worked on the questionnaire engine: the forms patients fill in, and the manager Kaiku used to deploy questionnaires to each clinic. Versioning let one shared questionnaire carry the content meant for each client. I was one of two engineers who built most of the manager.

The engine let Kaiku scale to a growing number of customers in different countries, each with its own localised questionnaires.
