myHedera is a mobile app, on both iOS and Android, where cancer patients record the side effects they experience during treatment. The data it collects makes clinical studies possible.

As one of three developers, I built the onboarding, the authentication and the dashboard. Signing in combines multi-factor authentication with an integration to the hospital's own systems, and the app supports offline use and push notifications throughout, all while handling patient health data in line with GDPR.

I still maintain the app, keeping it up to date with platform upgrades and monitoring it in production. It continues the work I started at Kaiku Health on [clinical questionnaires](#kaiku-clinical-questionnaires): helping patients report how they are doing between hospital visits.
