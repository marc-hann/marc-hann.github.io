Komu Homes runs apartment portfolios for their owners, from leasing and tenant service to maintenance and financials.

I co-founded the company as its CTO and was there for the first six months. In that time I set up the foundation for building the product the right way from day one: a serverless architecture on Firebase with a Vite frontend, a clear code structure, and engineering practices the team could keep building on as the company grew. With the team I built the MVP of the tenant app and shaped what the product should become.

Komu is still running today. After its inception I moved on to TietoEvry.
