Customer labs running Hedera Dx's tests go through a wet lab process before sequencing on Illumina machines. Each step involves calculations and careful handling of samples.

I led the development of a lab preparation application. It acts as a calculator for the wet lab process, tracks samples through each step, and exports the data the sequencer needs for the run.

Leading it meant much of the work happened before any code: scoping the product with lab specialists and turning deep domain expertise into clear requirements. It was as much a job of communication as of engineering.
