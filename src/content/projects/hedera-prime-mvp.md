Hedera Prime is sample-to-report software for cancer testing. It turns raw next-generation sequencing (NGS) data from tumour samples into clinical reports that match a patient's genomic alterations to approved therapies.

As Hedera Dx's first engineer, I took over the project from the consultants who had built the pilot, and developed the MVP together with them. I worked on every part of it, end to end. The hardest part was the workflow itself: the domain is complex, and scoping an MVP out of the pilot took real effort.

The MVP went on to win the first customers, and almost everything I built for it still runs in today's Prime: a CE-IVD medical device certified under the EU's IVDR, which labs use to run somatic NGS tests in-house.
